# England 871 screenshot inventory

The six `england-871-*.png` files are genuine iPhone captures supplied for the
updated England 871 website. Each source is 1320 × 2868 pixels and includes its
native iOS status bar. The website therefore sets `screenshotHasStatusBar` and
does not add a second synthetic status bar.

| Project asset | Supplied capture | Screen | Website placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `england-871-home.png` | `IMG_3454.PNG` | Today home | Homepage hero |
| `england-871-reader.png` | `IMG_3455.PNG` | Eleanor Chronicle reader | Product hero and Reader gallery item |
| `england-871-people.png` | `IMG_3456.PNG` | People directory | People gallery item |
| `england-871-series.png` | `IMG_3457.PNG` | Stories & Series | Series gallery item |
| `england-871-map.png` | `IMG_3458.PNG` | Map with Northumbria selected | Map gallery item |
| `england-871-timeline.png` | `IMG_3459.PNG` | 871–1399 Timeline | Timeline gallery item |

The older, unprefixed screenshot assets are retained in the repository but are
not referenced by the updated presentation. The new captures and associated
pricing copy must not be merged into production until the corresponding app
release and UK App Store subscription prices are public.
